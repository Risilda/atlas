05 — why this page exists

Atlas belongs to the first generation that will never remember a world without artificial intelligence.

The machines will keep getting smarter all around him — that part is already out of anyone's hands. What is still entirely in our hands is the human we raise beside them: how curious he stays, how kind he learns to be, how stubbornly he delights in the world. We're not preparing Atlas for a future with AI in it. We're raising the person who decides what AI should be for.

— his humans, watching the weights update daily
